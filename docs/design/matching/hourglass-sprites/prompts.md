# 沙漏精灵图生成记录

使用内置 imagegen，透明背景。参考 `docs/design/07-matching.png` 中的沙漏，属于生成重建素材，不是原稿无损切图。

## 第一版

Use case: stylized-concept. Production game animation sprite sheet, transparent RGBA. Reference image is a style and shape reference: use ONLY the brass hourglass in the center of the matching screen, exclude the broad circular platform and timer. Generate a clean exact 4 columns by 4 rows sprite sheet of 16 equal square cells, 2048x2048 total. No text, numbers, grid lines, backgrounds or extra props. Same orthographic camera, scale, lighting and centered pivot in every cell. Each hourglass occupies no more than 76% of the square cell so tilted frames never clip. Ornate polished antique brass frame with two supports, symmetrical circular caps, clear hourglass glass and golden sand, warm amber lighting, detailed realistic casual fantasy game rendering matching reference. Frame order left to right top to bottom: frames 1-8 upright stationary, upper sand progressively drains from 80% to 5% through a thin continuous falling stream, lower heap grows correspondingly, preserve total sand volume. Frames 9-16 smooth counterclockwise 180 degree turnover: angles 10,30,55,80,105,130,155,180 degrees. Crucial physical rule: during turnover sand is NOT glued to the glass or rigidly rotated with it. Gravity always points toward bottom of the image, free sand surfaces remain horizontal in screen space, grains avalanche down the inner walls to the lowest available part of the tilted glass, conserve volume. End frame upright with upper chamber refilled and lower nearly empty, physically ready to start next cycle. Consistent geometry, no morphing. Actual transparency outside each object and translucent empty glass. This is a usable sprite sheet not a conceptual diagram.

## 沙量连续性修正

Use case: precise-object-edit. Correct the attached 4x4 transparent hourglass animation sprite sheet. Preserve its 4x4 equal cell layout, brass design, camera, object scale, centered registration and genuinely transparent background. Fix temporal sand continuity only: frame 8 (row2 col4) upper chamber should be almost EMPTY, lower chamber contains 90% of sand. Frames9,10,11 (row3 cols1,2,3) must retain that SAME distribution: original upper chamber almost empty, original lower chamber holding almost all sand; do NOT refill upper chamber on starting rotation. At frame12 (row3 col4, near horizontal) sand remains pooled in original lower chamber toward its world-down glass wall. Frame13 (row4 col1) original lower chamber now tilted above neck, sand begins sliding down its inner wall toward the neck, slight small stream to other chamber. Frames14,15 sand settles in the now upper bulb with flat screen-horizontal sand surface, only a small amount has reached now lower bulb. Frame16 ends symmetrical upright with 80% sand in upper chamber and20% lower, matching frame1. Every free sand surface must be horizontal relative to image gravity, NEVER glued to rotating vessel. Sand may settle at angle of repose on slopes, conserve total sand volume across every frame. No text or labels or gridlines. Remove any red colored fringe/noise around objects. Clean semi-transparent antialiasing, transparent glass and background. Keep shell sizes consistent across frames.

采用 generated-v2.png。原始输出实际尺寸为 1254×1254；按实际行距裁切，再统一中心和画布，不直接假设生成器严格遵守 2048×2048 网格。运行时不再旋转外壳或缩放沙堆，只切换序列帧。
